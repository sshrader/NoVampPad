      From idea to live in a few minutes

      1. Design itPick a name, upload a logo, choose an accent color and how sharp or round the corners are. A live preview shows your page as you go.
      2. Set your cutChoose what share of creator fees comes to you. The coin's launcher always keeps at least half.
      3. Share itSign once with your wallet and your page is live. Anyone can launch a coin on it, and fees flow to you on-chain.

      Fees, split on-chain

      Every trade on a pump.fun coin pays a creator fee. On a pad, that fee is shared three ways by pump.fun's own fee-sharing, so nobody has to trust anybody to pay out. The split is written on-chain when a coin launches and nobody can change it afterwards, not even us.

      • The coin's launcher keeps everything that is left, and always at least half.
      • The pad owner gets the share they chose when they built the pad: from 0% up to 40%.
      • {{name}} gets 10% of every coin's fees.

      For example, if a coin earns 1 SOL in creator fees on a pad with a 20% owner share: the launcher gets 0.70 SOL, the pad owner 0.20 SOL, and {{name}} 0.10 SOL.

      Fees wait on the coin until someone presses Send out fees on its page. Anyone can press it, and it always pays the wallets in the split, never the person who pressed. On a pad with a pool pair other than SOL, the same split is paid in that token. Creator fees are only a slice of each trade, not the trading volume.

      Good to know

      Is this real money?

      Not yet. This site is running on devnet, Solana's test network, so every coin and every SOL is play money.

      Is this real money?

      Yes. Every buy, sell and launch uses real SOL. Only spend what you can afford to lose.

      Do you hold anyone's funds?

      No. Your wallet signs everything, and fees are paid by pump.fun's fee sharing straight to the wallets in each coin's split. This site never holds keys or balances.

      What does building a pad cost?

      Nothing to build. When someone launches a coin on your pad, they pay the network fees and the small rent for the new accounts.

      What can't I build?

      Pads that copy a real person, brand or project are not allowed, and names are checked. A pad that breaks the rules can be taken down.

      How do I get paid?

      Anyone can press Send out fees on a coin's page. The money always goes to the wallets in that coin's split, which includes yours.
